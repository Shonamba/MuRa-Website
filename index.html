The Work

MudduRamana Manasu

ಮುದ್ದುರಾಮನ ಮನಸು — The Heart of Beloved Rama

MudduRamana Manasu is a collection of 1100 four-line verses in the grand tradition of Kannada philosophical poetry.

Organised across 18 chapters from ಗುರುವೆನಗೆ ಶ್ರೀರಕ್ಷೆ to ಮೌನ ಸುಂದರ ಭಾಷೆ, the work moves through the full range of human experience.

The Poet

K.C. Shivappa

ಕೆ.ಸಿ. ಶಿವಪ್ಪ — Poet, Philosopher, Kannada Voice

K.C. Shivappa is the author of MudduRamana Manasu, a distinguished contribution to modern Kannada literary tradition.

His verses move between classical Kannada resonance and everyday clarity — making profound thought accessible to all.
