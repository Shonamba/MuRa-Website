About MudduRamana Manasu

MudduRamana Manasu — literally "The Heart (Mind) of Beloved Rama" — is a remarkable collection…

The work is organised into 18 chapters…

Each verse consists of four lines following classical Kannada prosodic conventions…

The title's invocation of Muddu Rama — Beloved Rama — anchors the work…

✍️

About K.C. Shivappa

K.C. Shivappa (ಕೆ.ಸಿ. ಶಿವಪ್ಪ) is the author of MudduRamana Manasu…

The verses of MudduRamana Manasu reflect a lifetime of reflection…

Shivappa's language moves fluidly between the richness of classical Kannada vocabulary…

The poet's choice of Muddu Rama — a name that carries both devotional warmth…

🌿

The Tradition: Kannada Philosophical Verse

Karnataka has a long and distinguished tradition of four-line philosophical verse…

The four-line form demands extraordinary compression. Every word must carry weight…

MudduRamana Manasu belongs to this tradition. It carries the DNA of the Vachana and the Kagga…
